✈️ Airline Passenger Satisfaction Analysis

Turning passenger data into actionable insights using Python, SQL, and Business Intelligence tools.

📌 Project Overview

Airline passenger experience plays an important role in service quality and customer satisfaction. This project analyses 103,904 passenger records to explore satisfaction levels, evaluate airline service ratings, examine passenger and travel characteristics, and understand flight delay patterns.

The project demonstrates an end-to-end data analytics workflow, from data preparation and SQL-based analysis to exploratory data analysis and dashboard-driven reporting.

🎯 Business Objectives

Measure passenger satisfaction: Examine the distribution of satisfied, neutral, and dissatisfied passengers.

Evaluate service quality: Compare passenger ratings across airline services.

Explore passenger segments: Investigate satisfaction patterns across customer types and travel characteristics.

Analyse flight delays: Summarise departure and arrival delays.

Communicate insights: Present analytical findings through charts, KPI summaries, and interactive dashboards.

💡 Key Takeaways

Passenger satisfaction: The reported satisfaction rate was 43.33%, while 56.67% of passengers were neutral or dissatisfied.

Service quality: Inflight Wi-Fi received a lower average rating than seat comfort among the reported service indicators.

Operational insights: Average departure and arrival delays were summarised to examine flight punctuality patterns.

Data-driven reporting: Combining analytical tools and visualisations helps make passenger experience findings easier to interpret.

These findings describe patterns in the analysed dataset; they do not establish causal relationships.
